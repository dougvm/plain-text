# Setting up MFA
As somebody who has spent the last decade teaching people how to set MFA up AND learning about how to set it up, I know first hand how much struggle, confusion, and overall human emotion that is evoked from setting up, troubleshooting, and being obstructed by MFA can be.

Unfortunately, I can only help people who reach out to me one way or another OR whoever may happen to receive or see my advice. Hopefully this helps with the latter!

<sub>**==WARNING:==** Provided advice is prone to the world's constantly changing tech trends, regulations, and research. The important to know regarding security is that it will always be a cat and mouse game, much like trying to secure a physical location can be against nature and the weather. </sub>
