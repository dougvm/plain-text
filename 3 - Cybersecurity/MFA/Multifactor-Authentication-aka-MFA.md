# Multifactor Authentication
Commonly referred to as two-factor authentication or 2FA, due to it having been initially rolled out less securely where the emphasis was on making it seem less of a daunting task.
MFA is the world's current best defense against getting hacked and is most people's primary cyber defense - while simulteaonously being their main enemy  often due to the inconvenience.

To understand how MFA is used day-to-day for digital security, it is often best to think of this as realistically and logically as possible. For example, you should look at your digital accounts like your physical home. 

1. Your door is how you get in and out of your house, you lock it with a key so others can't just walk up and open it. 
1. Much like a key gives you, *and whoever you share your house keys with*,  access to your home; the same can be said for your various forms of authentication - which give you (and those who you share your MFA methods / digital access with) access to your accounts.
1. The most basic form of authentication that most people know about are passwords.
