# plain-text: A Guide to Understanding Modern Technology.
> *Have you or someone you know been struggling with technology?*  
> *Perhaps you're scared of getting hacked, losing all of your money, and don't know how to defend yourself enough anymore?*  
> *Find yourself double-checking every email, every link, every call, just in case it's a scam?*  
> *Feel like keeping up with new apps and platforms is basically a part-time job now?*  
> *Worried about what your kid is seeing online — but not sure where to even start looking?*  

If any of these sentiments apply to you, I hope this finds you well and I'm beyond glad that you're reading this! My name is Doug Metz, I am a self-proclaimed nerd who has been using technology since my family shoved me in front of a PlayStation when I was a child in the early 2000s.  

Ever since then, I have spent nearly every year of life learning how to become, what most people would call a wizard — someone fluent in the language of the technology that now runs our lives. Throughout these years, I have helped countless people learn how to use computers and devices of all kinds... much to my own dismay as someone who has had to help their family with fixing anything that has electricity flowing through it one way or another! 

**Fun fact**: this reliance on people who know more than you do even happens amongst us nerds due to how many different subsections of knowledge that exist in the modern day computing world. I would not be where I was today if I had not been informed by the people who came before me, much like the entire computing industry itself. 

This guide is free and open to everyone — no account, no paywall, no catch. Skip straight to whatever's on your mind using the list below. The rest will be here when you need it.
